# OpenCrumb Product Brief

## One-line description

OpenCrumb turns bread recipes into scalable baker’s-percentage formulas serious bakers can trust.

## Audience

OpenCrumb is for:

- serious home bakers
- sourdough and bread-focused bakers
- small-batch bakers
- people who repeat doughs and care about consistency
- people who scale recipes, track hydration, and use preferments

OpenCrumb is not for:

- casual recipe collecting
- meal planning
- nutrition tracking
- social cookbook sharing
- generic AI chat

## Platform

- iPhone only for v1
- Native Apple feel
- Swift / SwiftUI direction
- Local-first
- No account required
- No backend assumed

## R1.0 scope

R1.0 is:

```text
Import → Formula → Scale
```

In scope:

- in-app browser recipe import
- recipe detection
- recipe-to-formula preview
- formula library
- formula detail view
- baker’s percentages
- hydration / yield summary
- preferments / levains / soakers / multi-stage formulas
- manual formula entry
- scaling by total dough weight
- scaling by ingredient constraint
- recalculating after a mis-weigh
- subscription paywall after trust is earned

Out of scope for R1.0:

- production scheduling
- backplanning
- timers
- attention queues
- team collaboration
- accounts
- sync
- backend
- Android
- social features
- chat interface
- AI-generated math

## Core aha moment

The aha is not “AI imported something.”

The aha is:

> My real recipe just became an organized, believable formula I might trust for my next bake.

The preview must feel credible, editable, and under the baker’s control.

## Positioning

OpenCrumb should be positioned as:

> A serious baking tool that turns recipes into formulas you can trust, scale, and bake from.

Avoid positioning it as:

- AI recipe app
- generic recipe manager
- meal planner
- productivity dashboard
- SaaS workflow tool

## Business model

- Subscription
- £2.99/month
- £14.99/year
- 3 free imports before paywall
- preview before paywall

Paywall principle:

> Earn the right to ask for money by showing the user their own recipe transformed into something useful.

## Design feel

The app should feel:

- calm
- precise
- legible
- practical
- trustworthy
- native to iPhone
- useful rather than decorative

Avoid:

- neon AI aesthetics
- glassy SaaS dashboards
- hype copy
- fake precision
- clutter
- tiny text
- generic recipe-card visuals

## Trust principles

- Deterministic math, never AI-guessed scaling
- User reviews before saving
- Honest uncertainty in imports
- No account required
- No ads
- No tracking
- Data stays on device except explicit import/extraction work
